Understanding Shares and Exchanges

A stock represents fractional ownership in a corporation. Public companies list their shares on authorized exchanges where participants review financial performance, earnings reports, and macroeconomic indicators.

Portfolio Diversification & Risks

Diversification involves spreading capital across multiple asset classes to manage sector-specific concentration risk. All equity investments carry market volatility risks, and past performance is not indicative of future results.
